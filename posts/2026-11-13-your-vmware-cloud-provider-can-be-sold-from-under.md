If your VMware workloads run at a hosting provider, your contract can be sold to another company without your say.

In October 2026, IBM was reported to have sold the contracts of hundreds of its cloud VMware customers to 11:11 Systems, which plans to move them to VCF 9. With Broadcom's partner changes and VCF 8 support ending, dates set outside your organisation now shape your options. A migration plan that depends on one provider and one timeline is not enough; you need options and an exit you have tested.
