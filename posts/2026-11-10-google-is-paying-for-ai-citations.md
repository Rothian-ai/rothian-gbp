Google is now paying publishers for AI citations. Brands should read the signal

Google’s new AI contribution pilot pays publishers when their content shapes answers in Gemini, AI Overviews and AI Mode.

What it means: Google now values a page by whether it shaped the AI answer, not only by whether it earned a click. Buyers increasingly build their shortlists inside those answers, so a brand that is not cited may never get the visit, and standard analytics will not show it.

What brands can do:
• List the 30 or so questions buyers ask before choosing a supplier.
• Measure your share of answer: run them across the main AI assistants each month and note whether you are mentioned, cited and described accurately.
• Make key pages easy to extract: a direct opening answer, specifics, FAQs and structured data.
• Keep your name, services and locations consistent across your site, LinkedIn and directories.
• Earn third-party mentions, which assistants weigh more than another post on your own site.
