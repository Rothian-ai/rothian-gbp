Google is adding more links to its AI answers. Whether any of them point to you depends on your pages, not on Google.

In October 2026, Google described more inline links in the AI answers in Search, a test that highlights one or two official pages for a task, and clickable creator names in AI Mode. Several of these are tests with no dates or regions, and Google gave no click data. The pages that earn citations state plainly what you do, for whom and what to do next, and only your own tracking will show whether the links bring visits.
