Buying an ad in ChatGPT won't make ChatGPT recommend you.

OpenAI is testing labelled ads beside images generated in ChatGPT, shown first to US users on the Free and Go plans. The format is small, but AI assistants are now an advertising channel, and OpenAI says ads do not affect its answers. B2B teams need to manage paid and organic presence in AI answers as separate jobs, and measure both.
