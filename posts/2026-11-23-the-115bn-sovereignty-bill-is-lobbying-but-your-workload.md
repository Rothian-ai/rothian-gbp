A lobby group's €115bn sovereignty bill is a scenario. Your own bill depends on how portable your workloads are.

A study commissioned by CCIA Europe puts the cost of the EU's proposed Cloud and AI Development Act at €115bn over ten years, more than six times the Commission's upper estimate. Treat the figure as advocacy, but note that 69% of it is code rewriting and re-architecture. Classify your workloads by sensitivity and by dependency on provider-specific services, and you will know your own number.
